title: 1933 -1934 Ford Digital Instrument System
---
Your 1933-34 Ford gets bright vacuum fluorescent readouts in blue or teal for speed, rpm, oil pressure, water temp, fuel and volts. The message centers add an odometer, two trip meters, a 0-60 timer and quarter-mile time and trap speed.

A billet aluminum dash housing made for the '33-34 Ford comes with it. The glove box cover, center panel and glove box handle are sold separately, and the covers may need some modification. A complete kit option adds a VFD clock and the glove box cover with handle. A metric version is a special order.

Water temperature and oil pressure sensors are in the box, plus a speed sensor that replaces a Ford or GM style speedometer cable. Pushbuttons you mount within reach handle calibration and scroll the message centers.
