title: 1969 Camaro RTX Instruments

With the key off, it looks like the factory '69 cluster: big speedometer, big tach, stock layout and face styling. Turn the key and LED bar gauges light up for oil pressure, coolant temp, voltage and fuel. Two color message centers show what you choose, from trip meters and miles to empty to 0-60 times and quarter-mile trap speed.

It drops into your stock or reproduction plastic bezel (sold separately) and uses an OEM or reproduction instrument carrier. The stock lenses and indicator rings are replaced by matte black aluminum trim rings, included in the box. A metric version in km/h and Celsius is a special order.

The housings are CNC machined and the electronics are designed and built in-house in Sioux Falls. Everything wires to one control box, so install and setup stay simple.
