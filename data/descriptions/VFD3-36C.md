title: 1935 Chevy Master/1936 Chevy Digital Instrument System
---
Bright digital readouts replace the gauges in your 1935 Chevy Master or 1936 Chevy. This Series III system uses vacuum fluorescent displays that are easy to read, in blue or teal. You get six gauges, and the message centers add an odometer, two trip meters, a 0-60 timer and quarter-mile time and trap speed.

The kit includes a billet aluminum dash housing made to fit the 1935 Master and 1936 Chevy. Water temperature, oil pressure and speed sensors are included, each with a 10 ft harness. A matching glove box door is sold separately, with or without a VFD clock. A metric version is a special order.

You set it up with pushbuttons you mount where you can reach them. They calibrate the speedometer and fuel gauge and set the cylinder count. Later, the same buttons scroll through the message centers.
