# Dakota Digital redesign prototype

A working mock-up of dakotadigital.com, built from the "Dash Finder" design
(PDF + design export) and the live site's content. Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript:
no frameworks.

`dash-finder.js` (finder logic), `img/` (WebP images).

## Full catalog (shop/)

Every category and product on dakotadigital.com is rebuilt in `shop/`: 212 category pages and 811
product pages, with the live content (descriptions, specs, tabs, manuals, galleries, related
products), real prices, SKUs, versions (e.g. metric), option pickers (style, color, bezel...) and
add-on accessories with prices. Photos are copied into `img/shop/` as WebP.

Pipeline (run from the repo root; `<cache>` is any scratch folder):

1. `python tools/crawl_live.py <cache>`: crawls every category and product page (polite, cached).
2. `python tools/crawl_items.py <cache>`: fetches each product's price/option steps
   (authorized by the site owner).
3. `python tools/build_catalog.py <cache>`: writes `shop/*.html`, `tools/content/catalog.json`
   and the image list.
4. `python tools/fetch_images.py <cache>`: downloads and converts the photos.
5. `python tools/build_pages.py`: regenerates the site pages, links everything to the catalog and
   rebuilds the search index.
6. `python tools/verify_catalog.py <cache> [--refetch N]`: compares every generated page with its
   live page (titles, links, SKUs, prices, options, tabs, manuals, related products, text coverage,
   images) and writes `tools/content/verify_report.json`. `--refetch` re-downloads a random sample
   of live pages to confirm the crawl is still current.

Add to cart on catalog pages is a prototype: it confirms the selection and links to the product on
dakotadigital.com.

## Built with Dakota Digital

The homepage shows real feature builds from the live site (Ring Brothers, Kindig-It, Revology,
ICON and others), each tagged with the system it uses. "Put this dash in my car" loads that car
and system into the Dash Finder and saves the car to My Garage. Builds that match the saved car
move to the front with a "Your car" badge. Data lives in `index.html`, behavior in `builds.js`.

## Built to order

The product page says up front that most RTX systems are built to order: under the price, a
"Built to order in Sioux Falls" line turns the 8-10 week build estimate from the shipping policy
into real dates (computed in the browser from today). It links to a "From our shop floor to your
dash" timeline with factory photos, what happens if a date slips, how to ask for a ready-to-ship
kit, and the lifetime warranty. The desktop buy dock repeats the ship window.

## Buy dock

On the product page, once the buy box's Add to cart button scrolls out of view, a dock slides in
(bottom of the screen on phones, under the header on desktop). It mirrors the buy box live: the
gauge photo in the chosen backlight color, running total, item count and fit status for the saved
car, with Add to cart and Options buttons. Logic is in `buy-dock.js`; it reads the buy box rather
than duplicating its pricing.

## Compare all five looks

The product page ends with "Every system for the 1969 Camaro": GRFX, RTX, HDX, VHX and VFD3
side by side with real prices, photos and specs from the live product pages (Oct 2026).
Desktop shows five aligned columns; phones get swipeable cards. A link under the price jumps there.

## My Garage

Every page has a garage button in the header. Pick a car once (in the finder or from the
button) and the site remembers it in this browser:

- The finder opens on your car.
- Product pages say "Fits your 1969 Camaro", or warn when the kit is for a different car
  and link to what fits.
- The dealer quote form fills in your car.

## View it

Open `index.html` in a browser, or serve the folder so every page behaves the same:

    python -m http.server 5178

then go to http://localhost:5178.

## What is real and what is not

- Real: prices, SKUs, add-ons, specs, manuals and photos on the product page come from the live site
  (Oct 2026). Category links in the finder open real dakotadigital.com pages.
- Needs checking: the finder's fitment data (which years get which systems) comes from the design file.
- Placeholder: the 12 dealers are sample data from the design (ZIP 57107 only; other ZIPs link to the live locator).
- Not wired up: Add to cart and Send request only show a note. They need the store's cart and dealer data.
