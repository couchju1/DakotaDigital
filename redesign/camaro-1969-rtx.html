Before you buy

Does it fit my original bezel?

Yes. It drops into a stock or reproduction plastic bezel. If yours is cracked, ask us about a new one.

Can I reuse my instrument carrier?

Yes. The system uses an OEM or reproduction carrier. Only add ours if yours is missing or damaged.

Can I change the lighting color?

Yes. The LED backlighting is adjustable, with over 30 colors and separate day and night themes, so you can match the interior or keep it close to factory.

Will it show my gear position?

Yes, with the GSS-3000 gear position sensor for automatic transmissions. Add it above.
